# Groups and events

## Join a discussion

Open **Groups**, a community link, or a community found through **Discover**.
Follow or request membership using the controls on its page. Following a group
does not grant moderation rights; the group's posting rules still apply.

Create a thread from the group page. Threads have a title and can include an
external link and body text. Replies, votes, reports and locks belong to that
community's discussion. A locked thread may remain readable while accepting no
new replies. Remote groups also enforce their own policies.

Owners and moderators use the group's management links for membership,
moderation and reports. Instance-wide suspension and domain policy belong to
instance administrators. Group-only moderators do not get unrestricted access
to the administrative console. A video channel is a different kind of managed
actor; see [channels](channels.md).

## Organize an event

Choose the Event post type. Enter the title and description, start/end times,
and a place or online meeting link. Check the time zone shown by the form,
especially across daylight-saving changes. You can indicate capacity and a
tentative event, and choose open attendance, approval, invitations or external
registration. Events cannot use ordinary scheduled-post publication.

Publish the event and share its link. The event's attendance controls let
readers respond; approval and invitation modes require the organizer's action.
As organizer, use **Attendees** to review pending requests and accept or decline
them. External registration happens at the linked service, under its rules.

If the event will not happen, use **Cancel this event**. The post stays visible
with its cancellation and attendees are notified. Editing or cancelling a
federated event propagates asynchronously; tell participants directly through
your usual channels if an urgent change must reach everyone.

Plamenu event participation does not imply support for every event client's
calendar, ticketing or guest-registration workflow. Planned video broadcasts
have their own [calendar links](live.md).
