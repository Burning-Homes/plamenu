# Notifications and mentions

**Notifications** collects activity such as mentions, follows, reactions and
requests. Use **Manage read state and notifications** to mark individual items
read/unread, delete them or mark all read. Opening the inbox does not mark all
its contents read. Check notification requests as well as the main inbox.

**Notification delivery** controls in-app, email and daily digest delivery for
each type. Email needs a confirmed address and working mail on your server;
email channels are off until you enable them. Email summaries link to a private
email-notifications inbox, including types you disabled in the in-app inbox.
Browser push also depends on browser permission and your push preferences.

The **Mentions** archive includes local, federated and website mentions. Filter
by source or non-public mentions. Clearing notifications does not erase this
archive; deleted, retracted and inaccessible content remains subject to access
checks. Music resources also have **Activities** pages for discussion of that
particular track, artist, album, collection or station.

Channel and group notifications identify the managed actor. Follow requests,
collaboration invitations and ownership offers link to their review forms.
An invitation grants no management access until accepted. A role in a channel
never makes somebody else's private reply readable.

If notifications are missing, check delivery preferences, requests, blocks and
mutes, then email confirmation or browser permission. **Live notifications** is
an optional interface setting; see its [known navigation limitation](../KNOWN_ISSUES.md).
