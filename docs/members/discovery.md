# Search and Discover

Use **Search** for people, posts and hashtags already known to your server.
Paste a full public post URL or account handle to resolve something remote.
A missing result can mean the source is unavailable, access is restricted, or
your server blocks it; search is not a complete index of the fediverse.

**Discover** offers People, Posts, Communities, Videos, Servers and Topics from
sources configured by your administrators. Choose a source, search term,
language or sort order to narrow the catalogue. **Open profile**, **Open post**
and **Open video** resolve the selected item for use in Plamenu; **View original**
visits its source website. Opening a result does not follow its author.

**New to you** uses your discovery viewing history. Use **Include seen items**
or **Reset viewing history** when you want to revisit suggestions. This history
is separate from video watch history and music listening history. **Hide**
dismisses a suggestion; use account blocking for a broader safety decision.
**Next page** remains available without automatic loading.

**Explore connections** follows authors, topics and public curated collections
already represented in the catalogue. Save up to 20 topics for later. Saved
topics are private and do not change your account follows.

A normal catalogue search runs on this server. **Search wider** asks you to
select remote servers before sending them your query. Those servers can see
the query and this instance's request. Results can be incomplete or unavailable;
this does not grant access to restricted posts or private profiles.

Paused updates, exhausted request allowance and empty catalogues are different
states. The page explains the current state. Operators can adjust acquisition
in [Discovery administration](../operators/discovery.md).

For a specific music site's catalogue or podcast website, use the search/link
box in [Music](music.md). For videos already resolved on your instance, use the
[video library](video.md). These views serve different catalogues.
