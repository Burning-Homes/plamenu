Plamenu accessibility review

19 October 2026 · Local changes for manual review. Initial baseline: a9a57424. Later comparisons use the prior implementation of each form; executable/source evidence is linked from the audit. These comparisons cover every visible change in the completed iterations.

Standard viewports: 1440 × 900 and 320 × 800. Landscape comparisons use 800 × 360 and 800 × 320. Most screenshots are full-page captures; focus comparisons can show a viewport or an identified crop. Fixed navigation appears at its viewport position. Open images for full size. Raw JSON error responses can use the browser’s wider fallback layout; original captures are preserved.
